# INC-2291 — queue-svc fetch timeout during sweep prep (RESOLVED)

Severity: S3 · Opened: 2026-06-02 · Resolved: 2026-06-03 · Owner: infra (M. Duarte)

## Summary

`queue-svc` intermittently timed out serving sweep queue manifests on
2026-06-02 (3 occurrences, max 41s). Root cause: connection pool exhaustion
after the 2026-06-01 pod autoscaler change.

## Resolution

Pool size raised 32 → 128, autoscaler floor restored. No recurrence since
2026-06-03. No impact on probe execution or probe results.
